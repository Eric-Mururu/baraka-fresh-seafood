About Baraka Fresh Seafood

A family business supplying fresh seafood along the Watamu coast.

Who We Are

Baraka Fresh Seafood is a family-owned business that has supplied fresh fish, prawns, crabs and squid along the Watamu coast since 2015.

We supply hotels, restaurants and households in Watamu and Kilifi. Our focus is simple: fresh seafood, fair prices, and an easy way to order.
